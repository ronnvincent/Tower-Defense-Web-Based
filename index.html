TOWER DEFENSE

⚔️ DSALGO BASTION EDITION ⚔️

TACTICAL FIELD MANUAL

OBJECTIVE

Defend your Realm Core Base (20 HP) against 6 incoming waves of invaders emerging from the Abyssal Portal. Earn gold coins by defeating enemies and spend coins to construct defenses.

TOWER TYPES

  • Archer Tower ( 60): Fast, balanced single-target ballista. Upgradable to level 3.
  • Cannon Battery ( 110): Heavy mortar firing ballistic shells with area-of-effect splash damage.
  • Frost Spire ( 85): Chills enemies, slowing movement speed by up to 60%.
  • Road Barricade ( 30): Placed directly on the road to cut graph edges, forcing enemies to reroute via BFS. Cannot seal off the only remaining path.

DATA STRUCTURES & ALGORITHMS IN ACTION

  • Circular Queue (O(1)): Powers wave spawning and the BFS search frontier without Array.shift() penalties.
  • Adjacency List Graph: Represents walkable road cells and orthogonal movement connections.
  • Breadth-First Search (BFS): Computes shortest paths, verifies barricade feasibility, and dynamic enemy rerouting.
  • Student Merge Sort (O(n log n)): Determines the exact ranking of local leaderboard scores.
