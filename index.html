TACTICAL FIELD MANUAL

OBJECTIVE

Pick a battle on the world map and defend your Realm Core Base (25 HP) against that level's waves of invaders emerging from the Abyssal Portal. Each level pays its own starting gold (220 / 240 / 260) and ends with a unique boss. Win to earn 1–3 stars, bank gold and gems, and unlock the next stage — the first victory also opens Endless Siege.

TOWER TYPES

  • Ballista Tower ( 60): Fast, balanced single-target fire. Upgradable to level 3 with two specialisations.
  • Cannon Battery ( 110): Heavy mortar firing ballistic shells with area-of-effect splash damage.
  • Frost Spire ( 85): Chills enemies, slowing movement speed by up to 60%.
  • Hammerforge ( 120): Heavy hammer slams that detonate for explosive splash. Branches into the armour-melting Emberforge or the long-reach Thunderforge.
  • Runestone ( 135): Magic rune bolts that ignore 75% of armour — the answer to Iron Brutes and shielded elites. Upgrades into the Void Lance (true damage) or the Arcane Nova (splash).
  • Banner Keep ( 125): Support keep: every tower in its aura hits harder and further. Specialise into the Great Warbanner or the gold-generating Muster Camp.
  • Palisade ( 30): A solid wall on the road. Enemies will not walk around it — they stop and break it down, which buys your defences the time they need. Reinforce it for extra HP and thorns damage. It can only be planted on the lane the horde actually marches.

CHAINS, EARLY CALLS & THE DAILY

  • Kill chains: kills inside 2.6 s of each other build a chain — 3 kills pays ×1.25, 6 pays ×1.5, 10 pays ×2 on gold and score. A leak (an enemy reaching the core) or the end of the wave breaks it, and the wave pays a bonus for your best chain.
  • Early call: while the last stragglers are still walking, START WAVE becomes CALL EARLY +Ng. It starts the next wave on top of the leftovers and pays 6–70 gold depending on how many are left — a tempo trade, not a free button.
  • Daily Challenge: the menu button starts one date-seeded battle with a rule twist (extra gold, a fragile core, a tougher horde…). The same day is the same challenge for everyone; clearing it pays gems and keeps your 🔥 streak alive.
  • Forge & blessings: gems from wins, dailies and achievements buy permanent relics in THE FORGE. In battle, the pause card's BLESSINGS trades gold for an immediate swing — damage, gold, a core repair or faster firing.
  • Endless: every 5 waves pays a checkpoint (gold + gems) and the wave badge shows your personal best, flipping to NEW BEST when you pass it.

MIRROR ARENA (1v1)

  • How a duel runs: host a room from DUEL · 1v1 LOBBY, send the five-character code to a friend, both press READY, then the host picks an arena. A three-second countdown opens both gates at once.
  • Fair by construction: the arena is mirrored 180°, so both halves are the same ground entered from opposite sides. Both players get the same 8 waves, the same bosses, 260 gold and 25 core HP. Relics, blessings and campaign progress are switched off.
  • Winning: the first core destroyed loses. If both cores survive all 8 waves, higher score wins — ties break on core HP left, then on clear time.
  • Watching your rival: pull the camera back to 0 to see the whole arena, and read the rival strip under the top bar (wave · points · core HP).
  • Leaving: quitting a live duel from the pause card is a forfeit, and the room closes immediately so nobody guards an empty arena.

CONTROLS

  • Camera: drag the battlefield to pan · mouse wheel or two-finger pinch to zoom · + / − / 0 for keyboard zoom and reset.
  • Build: click a dock card (or 1–7), then click a valid tile.
  • Free the battlefield: tap the FORGE DEFENCES handle above the cards (or press H) to fold the dock into a slim strip and back. Nothing pauses; the game remembers which way you left it.
  • Upgrade: click the gold badge floating above any defence.
  • Waves: Space starts the next wave or pauses · Esc cancels placement.
  • Codex: C opens the live algorithm drawer without stopping the battle — the pause card has a button for it too.
  • Leaving a battle: pause, then LEAVE BATTLE. It asks once more before dropping the run (finish the level to bank stars and rewards) and returns you to the world map, not the title screen.
  • Fullscreen: F or the ⛶ button toggles it. On a phone the game asks for fullscreen the first time you turn it sideways; in portrait the battle pauses behind a rotate prompt until the device is landscape again.

DATA STRUCTURES & ALGORITHMS IN ACTION

  • Circular Queue (O(1)): powers wave spawning and the BFS frontier without Array.shift() penalties.
  • Adjacency List Graph: represents walkable road cells and orthogonal movement links.
  • Breadth-First Search (BFS): computes the shortest route every enemy marches, and the codex visualises it live.
  • Merge Sort (O(n log n)): ranks the local leaderboard with a student-written sort.
