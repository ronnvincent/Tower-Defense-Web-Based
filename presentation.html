Simple Guide: Mechanics, Strategy, & How the Code Thinks

A direct, no-fluff explanation designed for high school students.

The Basics

How the Game Works

The Goal

  • Your base sits on the right side with 20 HP.
  • Monsters spawn on the left and march along the road to destroy your base.
  • If your base HP reaches 0, you lose.
  • Survive all 6 waves to win the game!

The Gameplay Loop

  • Monsters walk down the road.
  • Your towers shoot them down automatically.
  • Defeated monsters drop Gold Coins.
  • Use gold to buy new towers, upgrade them, or reinforce your base.
Defenses

Your Arsenal & The Fighting Base

The 4 Main Towers

  • Archer: Shoots fast arrows (great against fast runners).
  • Cannon: Fires bombs that blow up groups of enemies.
  • Frost Spire: Freezes monsters to slow them down.
  • Road Barricade: A roadblock that forces monsters to take the long detour.

The Bastion (Your Base Attacks Too!)

  • Your base isn't helpless—it fights back!
  • Level 1–2: Fires arrows at ground and flying monsters.
  • Level 3–5 Choices:
    • Storm Path: Strikes lightning from the sky to shock crowds.
    • Fire Path: Rains explosive mortar shells that leave fire on the road.
Strategy

3 Smart Tactics to Win

  • 1. The Detour Trap: Drop a cheap 30-gold barricade on the middle shortcut. Monsters have to walk twice as far, giving your towers double the time to shoot them!
  • 2. Freeze & Bomb Combo: Slow the front runner with a Frost tower so the monsters clump together, then blast the entire crowd with one Cannon bomb.
  • 3. Take Out the Healer: Plague Shamans heal nearby monsters. Switch your towers to target them first before they heal the boss!
Computer Science

How the Code Thinks (Algorithms Made Simple)

Pathfinding & Spawning

  • BFS (Monster GPS):
    Like Google Maps: Finds the fastest road to your base. If you place a barricade, it instantly finds a detour. (You cannot block the road completely!).
  • Circular Queue (The Lunch Line):
    Spawns enemies in First-In, First-Out order. Loops in a circle so the game never lags.

Map & Leaderboard

  • Graph (The Road Network):
    Connects road tiles like school hallways. Dropping a barricade cuts that hallway off the map.
  • Merge Sort (The Fair Leaderboard):
    Ranks high scores like sorting a deck of cards. Fast, 100% fair, and never glitches on ties.
Conclusion

Summary: Why It Works

  • Smart Strategy: Using barricades to make longer paths is the easiest way to win.
  • Base That Fights: Upgrading your base gives you a powerful safety net against boss waves.
  • Smooth & Lag-Free: Custom data structures keep the game running at a smooth 60 FPS.
  • Rock-Solid Code: Built from scratch with over 1,860 automated tests proving it works!
