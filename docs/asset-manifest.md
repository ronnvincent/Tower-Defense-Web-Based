## 2. Creatures — what the strips can really do

20 creature sheets ship idle, move and death.
**20 have no attack row and 20 have no hit row** — the
packs do not draw them. The game therefore plays the walk/jump pose plus a
pose-and-effect tell (recoil + strike flash at the wall) rather than pretending
a frame exists. Attack and hit are listed as `0` below instead of being left out.
