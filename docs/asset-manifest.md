## 2. Creatures — what the strips can really do

20 creature sheets ship idle, move and death.
**20 have no attack row and 20 have no hit row** — the
packs do not draw them. The game therefore plays the walk/jump pose plus a
pose-and-effect tell (recoil + strike flash at the wall) rather than pretending
a frame exists. Attack and hit are listed as `0` below instead of being left out.

## 5. Gaps and manual downloads

The sandbox can reach `github.com` / `api.github.com` / `codeload.github.com` /
`registry.npmjs.org` / `pypi.org` only. `opengameart.org`, `kenney.nl`, `itch.io`
and `craftpix.net` fail at the TLS handshake, so no pack below was fetched this
pass. They are listed with the licence that was verified from the source page,
and the step a human has to take:

| pack | official page | licence | what it adds | manual step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tower defense slime enemy (Tisroc) | <https://opengameart.org/content/tower-defense-slime-enemy> | CC0 | idle / walk / hurt / death / attack PNGs + one combined sheet | Download the zip, unzip into `art-source/enemies/`, then re-run `scripts/build_spire_assets.py` to repack 9-row strips. |
| Tiny Swords (Pixel Frog) | <https://pixelfrog-assets.itch.io/tiny-swords> | CC0 | vanguard units with idle / run / attack rows (already used for the Vanguard) | Already vendored in `art-source/units/`; no download needed. |
| Tower Defense (Top-Down) (Kenney) | <https://kenney.nl/assets/tower-defense-top-down> | CC0 | 300 tiles/props/towers — static art, no animation rows | Optional: `npm run` fetch is not wired; download manually and keep the licence file next to it. |

Recorded documentation gap: the seven CC0 foley clips in `public/assets/audio/`
have no upstream page recorded in the repository (licence only). Everything else
above names its source.
